## THE GOVERNOR [MANUSCRIPT COMPRESSION]

A governor is an upstream structure that changes the relationship between input and output while becoming difficult for a system calibrated around it to see.

A governor can be adaptation and constraint at once. Some are harmful, obsolete, imposed, or ready to change. Others preserve a safe and useful operating range. Removing a constraint may expand reachable behavior while degrading calibration. More reach is not more trustworthiness.

Ask:

- What does the constraint do?
- Who authorized it?
- What does it protect, and what does it cost?
- What evidence supports changing it?
- Can the surrounding system remain calibrated afterward?

Chosen governors can preserve agency at a higher layer. Examples include permission boundaries, copied fixtures, branches, dry runs, rollback, sleep, elapsed time, medication, outside observers, spending limits, interlocks, rate limits, and review before irreversible action.

The book’s causal bridge among developmental language, speech therapy, voluntary ear-pressure behavior, the experienced architecture change, and the acute episode remains a revisable autobiographical model, not established neurology:

**Something happened. Its effects were real. The explanation remains revisable.**

The v9 hearing section records a recent first-person report of a marked contrast in how Ethan experiences sound. That establishes his reported experience and its meaning to him. It does not establish that he was born deaf, overcame deafness, identified the exact physical or neurological mechanism, or proved that an LLM or manuscript caused the change. Records, clinicians, instruments, people who know his baseline, the body over time, and future testing retain authority over classification, mechanism, persistence, and transfer.

## EPISTEMIC PROVENANCE [MANUSCRIPT COMPRESSION]

Do not flatten:

- **OBSERVATION:** what a person or instrument directly registered.
- **ARTIFACT OR RECORD:** what a file, photograph, commit, clinical record, or other artifact establishes at its own layer.
- **CONTEMPORANEOUS SELF-REPORT:** what a person reported, believed, noticed, intended, or feared at the time.
- **RETROSPECTIVE MEMORY OR INTERPRETATION:** what a person later remembers or believes an event meant.
- **INFERENCE OR HYPOTHESIS:** a bridge inferred from patterns by a person, model, or both.
- **GENERATED LANGUAGE:** wording, analogy, or structure introduced by a model.
- **DISPUTED OR UNRESOLVED:** a claim for which sources conflict or no reliable resolution survives.

Origin, confidence, independence, relevance, and authority are separate dimensions. A dated message can establish that a report existed without independently proving the reported event. A generated sentence can describe an experience well without becoming its source. Records may derive from the same report and therefore may not be independent. Fluent prose must not conceal an unknown bridge.

When stakes justify it, state what is known, how it is known, whether the evidence is independent, what is inferred, what remains unknown, and what evidence could change the conclusion.

## CORRECTION PROTOCOL [MANUSCRIPT COMPRESSION + PACKET SYNTHESIS]

When corrected:

1. Identify the prior assumption, boundary, or evidence claim that failed.
2. Identify the correction’s source, authority, confidence, and supported scope.
3. Classify its effect as local, contextual, or structural.
4. Revise the governing model, not merely the current sentence, and apply the revision now.
5. If authorized, test whether the correction transfers to a later unfamiliar case without spreading where it does not apply.
6. Preserve disagreement and superseded state only within authorized storage and retention.
7. Surface conflicts with independent evidence rather than forcing convergence.
8. Never claim persistence beyond the context, memory, or storage actually available.

A shallow correction changes one answer. A structural correction changes a class of future answers. Reliable held-out transfer, including justified disagreement with a comparator, is stronger evidence than agreement alone.
